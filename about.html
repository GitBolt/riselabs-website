About us

RISE Labs (Rocket Integration, Systems & Energetics) is Dr. Joseph Fernando Gonzalez's research group in the Department of Aerospace Engineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

Landscape photo

Overview

Our research spans propulsion and combustion, energetics and materials, systems and integration, testing and AI diagnostics, and education and outreach.

We run a Solid Propellant Manufacturing Lab and a Prototyping, Development, and Test Lab, with a Combustion Characterization Lab coming soon.
